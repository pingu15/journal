Privacy Policy

Today: Daily Journal. Effective November 18, 2026.

Your journal is yours. Today has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't collect, see or sell anything you write or do in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code, and it never sends your data to us or anyone else. We have no way to read your journal.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Entries: what you write, and the photos and videos you add (copies are kept inside the journal).
  • Place and weather on an entry: where you wrote it (a place name and coordinates) and the weather there, if you allow location.
  • Place history: only if you turn on Track Visited Places. Your location is noted about every five minutes, in the background too, so a day can show the places you spent time at. It never leaves your iPhone and is never synced.
  • Settings: your preferences, kept on this iPhone.

Like other app data, all of this is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), which Apple or you control.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your entries, photos and videos are stored in your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. That data is in your Apple Account, protected by Apple, and we can't access it. Place history and settings never sync.

Apple services the app uses

Some features ask Apple's services for information. These requests go straight from your iPhone to Apple, never to us, and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Place names and search (Apple Maps): a location is sent to find its name, and what you type when searching for a place is sent to find matches. Maps are shown with Apple Maps.
  • Weather (Apple Weather): an entry's location and time are sent to look up the weather.

Permissions

Each one is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, and can be changed anytime in iOS Settings.

  • Location: to tag entries with a place, and (with Always) to note your place history in the background.
  • Photos: to show the photos you took on a day beside what you wrote, and in the widget. Your library is read, never changed or uploaded.
  • Calendars: to show a day's events. Read-only; events are never stored or changed.
  • Face ID: to lock the journal. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder and On This Day. They're scheduled on your iPhone and never include what you wrote.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Widgets read a small summary kept on your iPhone (your journal's name, which days you wrote, word and item counts), never your entries' text. Siri and Shortcuts can start a new entry; this runs on your device.

Sharing and export

When you share an entry or export your journal (PDF or Markdown), the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

  • Deleted entries go to Trash and are removed for good after 30 days, or sooner if you empty it.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Synced data can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete; all of it is in your control on your devices and in your iCloud. We don't sell or share personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
